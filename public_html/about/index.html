What we believe

A shower curtain is the most overlooked object you touch every day. We believe it should be honest — free of chlorine and odor, kind to the planet, and quietly beautiful enough to disappear.

Our journey of purpose

From one toxic liner
to zero compromise.

2019 · The catalyst

A liner that smelled of chemicals.

Our founder unrolled a new vinyl liner, caught that sharp plastic odor, and asked the obvious question nobody had answered: why does this have to be toxic?

2021 · The invention

Chlorine-free PEVA, perfected.

Eighteen months of material trials gave us a heat-welded, chlorine-free PEVA that’s odorless from the box and recyclable at the end of its life.

2023 · The evolution

Three grades, one obsession.

The magnetic weighted hem, rustproof grommets, and the Essential / Premium / HeavyDuty system turned a single liner into a range that fits every bathroom.

Today · The future

Zero compromise, by design.

We’re now a take-back program away from a fully circular liner — and we won’t stop until every shower has dropped the toxic plastic for good.
